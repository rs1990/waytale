## Use of the app

WayTale narrates landmarks as you travel, using GPS location to trigger
audio content. **Do not interact with the app while driving.** Narration is
designed to play passively; any on-screen interaction should happen only
when safely stopped or as a passenger.

## Content accuracy

Narration is drafted from Wikipedia and Wikidata using an AI system, then
reviewed before publishing. Content is tagged `verified`, `legend`, or
`mixed`:

- **Verified**: sourced directly from cited references.
- **Legend / mixed**: contains folklore or unverified claims, always
  labeled as such in the app. Treat this content as storytelling, not fact.

We aim for accuracy but do not guarantee it. Report inaccuracies to
[support contact — fill in].
