## What we collect

- **Location data.** WayTale uses your device's GPS location — including
  background location while a tour is active — to detect nearby landmarks
  and trigger audio narration. This is the app's core function; it does not
  work without location access.
- **Account data** (if you create an account): email address and a hashed
  password. We never store your password in plain text.
- **Usage data**: which landmarks you view or listen to, for the purpose of
  improving content and (for premium features) syncing your trip history.

## What we don't do

- We do not sell location data to third parties.
- We do not track your location when the app is not actively running a tour.
- Free-tier ambient narration does not require an account.

## Third-party services

- **Wikipedia / Wikidata**: narration content is drafted from these sources
  (see in-app attribution and `CC BY-SA 4.0` license notices).
- **Amazon Polly**: converts narration scripts to audio. No user data is
  sent to Polly — only pre-written scripts, at content-creation time, not
  per user request.
